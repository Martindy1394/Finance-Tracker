Telegram notifications

Money Tracker sends your reminders as messages in Telegram, a free chat app for your phone.

Telegram reminders aren't available yet: your admin still needs to set up the Money Tracker bot.

Set it up once — it takes about 2 minutes:

  1. Install Telegram on your phone. It's free on the App Store and Google Play. Open it and sign up with your mobile number. (Already have Telegram? Skip this.)
  2. Open the Money Tracker bot. Tap the button below — it opens a chat with the bot in Telegram.
    Open the bot in Telegram
    Using a computer? In Telegram on your phone, search for the bot, open it and send this code: —
  3. Tap Start at the bottom of the chat in Telegram.
  4. Come back here and tap Connect.
  5. You'll get “✅ Connected!” in Telegram. Done — tap Send test message below any time to check.
📱 Connected to via .

What you'll get on Telegram

  • 🔔 Bill reminders — on each bill's day and time (set them on the Bills page with ✏️), an early reminder if you choose one above, and a reminder the day after if it's still unpaid.
  • 📈 Budget alerts — when spending in a category reaches 80% or goes over.
  • 📝 Daily nudge — in the evening if you haven't recorded anything that day (optional).
  • 📊 Monthly summary — on the 1st: money in, money out and what's left. Your month is saved to the archive.
  • 🔐 Security messages — password reset codes, password changes and warnings about wrong-password attempts.

Not getting messages?

  • Allow notifications for Telegram in your phone's settings, and don't mute the bot's chat.
  • Blocked or deleted the bot? Open it again, tap Start, then Disconnect and Connect here.
  • Tap Send test message above to check that messages arrive.
  • Messages are sent by the computer that runs Money Tracker, so it has to be on and online.
  • To stop messages, switch off Send me reminders on Telegram or tap Disconnect.

Adding a new user

  1. Click Add user and enter their name and a username. A temporary password is made for you.
  2. Give them what's shown next — the address, their username and temporary password. Copy instructions puts it all in a message you can send.
  3. When they first log in, they choose their own password. Then they open Profile → Telegram notifications, which walks them through connecting their phone.
  4. Forgot their password? Use Reset password on their card to give them a new temporary one.
